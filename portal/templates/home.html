MAG, playable again.

mag-recomp is a native static recompilation of the PlayStation 3 shooter MAG for macOS, Windows and Linux. This site runs the community server it connects to, and the accounts that keep your player name yours.

How to play

  1. Bring your own copy. You need an unmodified disc dump of the retail v1.0 release (BCUS98110). Nothing from the game is ever downloaded or shared.
  2. Build it on your computer. Download the installer for macOS (Apple Silicon), Windows 10/11 or Ubuntu 24.04+ from the mag-recomp releases, choose your game folder and click Build MAG. It takes about an hour and needs around 20 GB of space.
  3. Pick your name and play. MAG connects to play.magtest.online by default. A controller is the recommended way to play.

The player guide covers first launch on each system, controls and settings.

Your account

Sign in with Google and claim your player name: nobody else can then play under it, and your characters follow you to any computer. Linking the MAG launcher to your account arrives in an upcoming release; until then the server still signs players in by name.

The projects

mag-recomp

The recompiled game client, its runtime glue and the one-button installer that builds MAG from your own copy.

mag-recomp-server

The replacement server: a Rust gameplay engine, the Medius lobby stack, and this accounts site.

ps3recomp

The PlayStation 3 static recompilation SDK that mag-recomp is built on.

Status

MAG builds and plays on all three systems, signs in and joins online matches on this server. It is not finished: rendering has known gaps, audio stutters, and frame rates vary by system while performance work continues.

Help and community

Questions, install problems and play sessions: the MAG Recomp Discord. Reproducible bugs go to mag-recomp issues, or server issues for problems with matches, sign-in or this site.
