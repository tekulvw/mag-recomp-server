Your account

One name, yours alone

Sign in with Google and claim your player name: nobody else can then play under it, and your characters follow you to any computer.

Linking the MAG launcher to your account arrives in an upcoming release; until then the server still signs players in by name.

How to play

  1. 01

    Bring your own copy

    An unmodified disc dump of the retail v1.0 release (BCUS98110). Nothing from the game is ever downloaded or shared.

  2. 02

    Build it on your computer

    Get the installer for macOS (Apple Silicon), Windows 10/11 or Ubuntu 24.04+ from the releases, choose your game folder and click Build MAG. About an hour and 20 GB.

  3. 03

    Pick your name and play

    MAG connects to play.magtest.online by default. A controller is the recommended way to play.

The player guide covers first launch on each system, controls and settings.

Status

MAG builds and plays on all three systems, signs in and joins online matches on this server. It is not finished: rendering has known gaps, audio stutters, and frame rates vary by system while performance work continues.

Help and community

Questions, install problems and play sessions: the MAG Recomp Discord. Reproducible bugs go to mag-recomp issues, or server issues for matches, sign-in or this site.
