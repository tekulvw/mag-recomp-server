# Acquisition dynamic body coverage and lifecycle

The human authorized complete live body coverage and remaining lifecycle handlers,
with checked integration and manual releases. Continue source/offline work; games,
deployments and released pin changes remain paused.

Implement a real owned dynamic-body snapshot/query rather than ActorGrid. Its
strict external catalog supplies capsule/sphere/rounded-box/hull geometry, every
registered root, body filters and source hashes. Coverage compares instantiated
entities against a separate complete authoritative roster; missing/extra entities,
unknown profiles, stale generations, unsupported shape/numerical limits fail the
whole query. Do not sort or deduplicate recipients. Include both chassis and
wheel roots and preserve the supplied root traversal order.

Use bounded convex support/distance queries with lower/upper distance bounds and
explicit uncertain-boundary errors. Native penetration is strict distance < sum
of radii; exact tangency is not penetration. Do not subtract radius from box core
extents. Supply the query's source-bound matrix; no host-trig substitution. Native
hull tolerances, broadphase order and numerical equivalence remain separately
qualified; replacement traversal order is explicit, not claimed native order.

Build a read-only Engine snapshot from its actual retained human/bot/ground
objects and current poses. Unspawned character bodies begin at zero; dead/mounted
characters remain included; mounted ground characters follow vehicle position.
Missing authoritative pose/template must fail, not omit an object. Current Engine
has no mine creation/registry; do not pretend a detached MineState owns live mines.
Catalog supports the extracted mine shapes for a future owned admission path.

Retain original data/extraction/native instruction evidence privately. Public
fixtures are invented. No full callback installation until actual authoritative
entry/damage/FSP and effect ordering are complete. Escort objects are distinct
from insertion APCs; never reuse APC identities, geometry or tuning for Escorts.
