# Operational Hospital blast damage

A source-bound Domination profile can supply `core.hospital_blast_areas` to
enable ordinary-frag and available hospital-rocket damage to operational
Hospitals. The option is absent/off for existing profiles. Ordinary frags work
without the other typed frag receivers, rockets or projectile contacts; current
retail frag metadata with authored penetration and the qualified world/material
inputs remain required. Rocket firing retains its existing prerequisites.

The maintained `tools/extraction/project-retail-hospital-blast-areas.py`
verifies the original executable, Main archive, selected operational collision
members and cached bounds. Its strict three-faction catalog binds those sources,
the loaded collision document and exact faction packs. Runtime classification
uses the current operational model pose. The family13 operational body uses
layer22; the distinct layer17 hulk is excluded. Archive spawn-class numbers are
separate from the existing Hospital replication identity.

The planner qualifies current mesh overlap and one target-excluding layer24
material ray, then applies the weapon's vehicle channel with zero prevention
reductions, native area1–6, armor and friendly scaling. The firing Hospital
remains eligible for its own rocket splash. All ordinary-frag terminals in one
event accumulate against one candidate and publish a final batched field18 with
the authenticated attacker, type10/area, encoded direction and explicit invalid
vehicle source word. The admitted projectile identity remains in audit.

The candidate installs only each Hospital's health, damage fields and damage
clock after the outer record. It preserves turret grants, aim, magazines and
commands, including same-event updates. The normal following Domination pass
handles critical decay, destruction, occupants, turret revocation, hulk changes,
schedules and rebuild. The blast planner does not advance that lifecycle early.

Publication follows the existing Hospital contract: same-world recipients must
have emplacement creation. A later creation contains current health. Ordinary
frag uses the existing bounded queue and recorded backlog-retirement policy;
its turret-aim budget counts actual Hospital recipients. Rockets include the
Hospital frame in their existing combined preflight for eligible recipients and
retain terminal retry after queue drain. Recorder refusal installs no candidate
health, terminal or publication.

One dose per Hospital per terminal, whole-target ray exclusion and the outgoing
invalid source word are explicit replacement policies. Native repeated callback,
self-filter and vehicle source-word parity remain unqualified, as does the
blocked-ray fallback. No generic logical-objective damage receiver is inferred.
Original-input and scripted Engine checks establish source behavior; native
client and live-match acceptance remain separate. Releases are manual after
the maintainer's live-match review.
