# Repair work, XP and contextual FRAGO

The qualified manual Ground, Hospital and Structural objective paths report accepted
repair work. Each credited callback adds f32(1 + the repairer's effective
RepairAmount modifier) to a separate actor work carry. At the inclusive whole
unit threshold, stat35 increases by the truncated units and retains the work
remainder. The unit counter describes work, not restored HP. The same recorded
transaction commits the accepted queue or float HP mutation, work state, stat
publication and optional immutable XP receipt.

Structural credit occurs after a successful contribution enqueue, before later
HP batching or completion. Acquisition and Domination reuse their existing
isolated character-effect candidate so recorder failure restores both repair
work and reward state. Current Domination assignment and range are sampled
before temporarily moving its runtime into the commit helper. A covered queue,
wrong route, rejected actor/tool, failed record or full reward outbox cannot
consume work or commit a hidden contribution.

Manual Hospital credit additionally requires a strictly positive authoritative
float HP change. It can earn credit even when the quarter-HP wire projection is
unchanged. If the model's remembered last attacker resolves to the repairer's
current actual team, healing succeeds but work/stat35/XP credit is suppressed.
An unresolved or opposing-team attacker does not suppress credit; saved faction
alone does not determine that relationship. A missing repaired-object position
omits that FRAGO position without suppressing base credit. The existing current
actor position may still qualify. Equipped-tool, life, owner and cooldown guards
remain on the existing manual route. One effective leadership snapshot supplies
both the repair amount and raw work modifier.

## Negotiation and settlement

Gameplay advertises `repair_rewards_v1`. Horizon emits optional character field
`repair_rewards_enabled` only to such a peer and retains the enabled value on its
reservation. It requires the selected retail catalog's finite nonnegative authored
`Awards.ExperiencePerRepairUnit`; explicit zero is supported and absence disables
new receipts. Only enabled gameplay emits `repair_units` with 1..65535 units.
Gameplay stat35 and the work carry remain independent of account XP opt-in.
New gameplay with an older Horizon receives no repair opt-in; new Horizon omits
the unknown field when paired with older gameplay.

Horizon multiplies whole repair units by the authored ratio using the existing
f32 fused shared XP carry. This XP carry is independent of repair's work carry.
The callback that supplies the units freezes current range context for the
ordinary positive whole XP generated at settlement, including carried fractional
XP from earlier kinds. Qualified actor OR repaired-object context uses the
existing squad tag/primary and inclusive XYZ predicate. Support FRAGO remains
separately gated by `frago_action_bonus_enabled`; its complete-catalog admission
conservatively also requires the revive rule at admission. After an enabled
admission, fresh repair settlement requires its own ratio and bonus coefficient;
removing an unrelated revive award does not revoke that retained opt-in. Base
repair XP works without the FRAGO coefficient. A true repair context without
the required admitted flag or its own fresh rules is rejected before mutation.

The existing atomic ledger saves base XP, XP carry, positive truncated bonus,
stat3 and the immutable receipt together. Exact replay precedes fresh authored
ratio availability and finalization checks. Gameplay publishes stat35 once at
the accepted work event; later XP ACKs do not add those units again. Bonus ACKs
retain existing absolute, nonregressing and current-round identity behavior.
The saved schema3 ledger accepts the new reward kind. An older Horizon reader
cannot read a store containing repair receipts, including receipts without
FRAGO; plan a compatible-store strategy before downgrading rather than deleting
immutable history.

## Lifetime and scope

The existing match-owned participation record retains repair work and ordinal
through ordinary life changes and same-character re-admission. A different
account or character in the same transport slot cannot inherit it; a fresh match
has fresh participation state. Retail construction and per-character sharing are
verified, while the replacement's retained rejoin behavior is an explicit local
policy. Server restart does not persist the in-memory match work counter.

Leadership AutoRepair passes no credited callback and remains rewardless.
Manual movable-Ground repair now has the [qualified live input path](manual-ground-repair.md). Full-repair,
subtype completion and leader tagged-objective counters are distinct zero-XP
retail paths and are not manufactured by this work producer. Other objective
FRAGO contexts and live-match/native presentation acceptance remain separate.
No original files or populated catalogs belong in public source. Releases remain
manual after live review; checked source integration is not a deployment.
