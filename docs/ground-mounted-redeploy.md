# Ground-mounted redeploy

An authenticated owned `KillSelf` request can redeploy a human from the existing
Ground passenger, driver and gunner routes. It resolves the current vehicle
allocation, completed creation, configured seat and unique occupancy before
planning terminal damage. Conflicting mount families and unbound seats refuse
without changing the character. Other mounted families retain their separate
support boundary.

A terminal redeploy uses the existing zero-health and state7 publication. The
recorded commit clears the actor's seat and self-owned pending effects, releases
driver ownership and revokes a gunner's equipment grant. Another human can use
the freed seat. The vehicle retains its current position and health; the player
uses the ordinary team spawn wave afterward. No geometry-dependent manual exit
or teleport is required to kill the owned character.

Original retail admits owned mounted self-kill and releases the actual occupant
while forcing state7. Its notification source is Unknown0/index-1, with the same
human as attacker and defender. This source is distinct from weapon metadata
and Falling1. Initial living self-kill can publish once to the current actual
squad. Downed give-up retains the original collapse notice and statistics.

The associated-vehicle flag samples state14 and a current qualified allocation
before release. Vehicle HP, operational state and occupant visibility do not
define that flag. The body mapping supplies the head predicate. Existing damage
remains available without optional combat body metadata, with no invented notice
flag. Other world-effect source admission is unchanged.

Synthetic native-event checks cover passenger/driver/gunner redeploy and seat
reuse, terminal wire state/HP, ownership revocation, stale/conflicting mounts,
owned-target checks, recorder refusal, on-foot notice accounting and downed
give-up without replay. Original-file checkers qualify native admission and
cleanup; controls, camera restoration, presentation and live delivery still
require a human live-match review. Releases remain manual.
